# CalFresh ABAWD rules: verification of the HourProof PRD

Checked 2026-09-25 against the PRD snapshot `docs/PRD-snapshot-2026-09-25.md`. Not legal advice. A caseworker or legal-aid advocate still has to review this before the pilot.

## How this was checked

Every source below was opened and read in full as raw text (curl + pdftotext). Search snippets were not counted as sources. All quotes are verbatim; `[...]` marks cuts.

When sources disagree, the **CDSS All County Letters (ACLs) and Information Notices (ACINs) control**, because counties must follow them. The most important one is **ACL 26-29, the CalFresh ABAWD Time Limit Handbook v3.0 (April 15, 2026)**. It supersedes the 2019 handbook as of June 1, 2026, and the PRD does not cite it. Two of the PRD's sources are out of date:

- **WCLP** (dated Aug 21, 2025) was written before CDSS issued its guidance. It gives the wrong start date (Feb 1, 2026), the wrong clock end (2029) and the wrong age range ("18-65").
- **LSNC** still mixes in some pre-H.R. 1 wording. It lists "living with a household member under 14" as a separate item, and it says homeless people are "considered unfit" under ACL 16-24, which CDSS has since narrowed.

## Changes the PRD needs

1. **Never say "exempt" to people who are working 20+ hours.** Add a fourth result: "You're under the rule, and you're meeting it right now. Keep proof and report if you drop." Only 30+ hrs/week or $217.50+/week gross leads to "likely exempt" (rows 8i, 15).
2. **Rewrite the child question.** Ask: "Is a child under 14 part of your CalFresh household (the people you buy and cook food with)?" Remove "caring for". A child who lives elsewhere does not count. Separately, add "Are you the parent of, or do you care for, a child under 6, even if the child lives somewhere else?" (rows 2, 8j).
3. **Add the missing exemptions:** 30+ hrs/week work, child under 6 (any household), CalWORKs work requirement compliance, ORR training at half-time, and the rule that 60–64 is not exempt by age alone (rows 1, 8j).
4. **Fix the college claim.** The threshold is half-time, not full-time. Say "likely exempt" only when the person is at least half-time **and** passes the student rule. For CCC/CSU/UC associate or bachelor's students, LPIE covers the student rule. Other students go to "ask your county" (row 14).
5. **Redo the months-used counter.** Start it at the user's first H.R. 1 screening (an application on or after June 1, 2026, or the first recertification due June 2026 or later). Count only full months the user marks as short of 80 hours with no exemption and no good cause. Never count partial months. Label the number "your estimate" and link to the official count on the BenefitsCal dashboard (row 5).
6. **Job search:** stop counting self-directed job search. Count job-search hours only when they are logged as part of a named E&T, WIOA or Trade Act program, and flag them when they reach half of the program hours. Allow a full 20 hrs/week of stand-alone WIOA job search (row 7).
7. **Workfare:** give it its own path (required hours = benefit ÷ highest minimum wage) and do not add it to other activity types (row 6b).
8. **Proof packet:** for each organization and month, include a CF 888-style block: organization, representative's name, address, phone, month, hours, ongoing vs one-time, and a **signature line**. This settles the PRD's open question about a supervisor signature. Also offer the blank official CF 888 PDF. Remove the claim that an in-app confirmation "is a collateral contact"; instead say "the county can call [supervisor, phone] to confirm" (rows 10b, 22).
9. **Alerts:** trigger when the **month-to-date average** falls below 20 hrs/week (80/month), not after one slow week. Show the 10-day reporting reminder only to users who say their H.R. 1 screening already happened. Exclude workfare hours (rows 11, 21).
10. **Regaining eligibility plan:** 80 hours in any 30 consecutive days that have **already happened**, then a new application with proof. Mention the one-time extra 3 consecutive months. Do not promise automatic reinstatement (row 13).
11. **Good cause button:** use the CDSS list (add "illness of a household member" and "Medi-Cal hardship"; say "household emergency" and "declared disaster"). State that the county decides one month at a time and that proof is not required but notes help (row 12).
12. **Veterans, homelessness, foster history, domestic abuse, addiction:** never say "exempt" for these. Send veterans to the disability-benefits question (any VA rating qualifies). Send the others to "ask your county to check if a health condition makes 20 hrs/week hard" (row 9).
